This repository contains coursework as well as a research project. Keep work scoped to the user's task.

For the research completion swarm, start at [`project/shortcut-study/SWARM_START.md`](project/shortcut-study/SWARM_START.md), then follow the extension's [`AGENTS.md`](project/shortcut-study/AGENTS.md).
